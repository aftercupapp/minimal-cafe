# Minimal Cafe

A high-contrast, text-based barista simulator designed for the **Minimal Phone** and e-paper devices. Manage your inventory, memorize recipes, and serve procedurally generated customers in this distraction-free simulation game.

## ✨ Key features

### 👨‍🍳 Become a minimal barista
* **Order fulfillment:** Interpret customer requests and build them layer-by-layer in the kitchen - minimally.
* **Recipe depth:** Master over 14 distinct recipes including Flat Whites, Dirty Chais, Espresso Tonics, and Matchas.
* **Dynamic customers:** Procedurally generated names, moods, and ASCII-style faces that react to your service quality.

### 📉 Economy & management
* **Inventory:** Ingredients are finite. Use your earnings to restock beans, milk, syrups, and ice from the wholesale shop.
* **Progress:** Climb the career ladder from **"Intern"** to **"Coffee God"** based on your total cash earned.
* **Real-time brewing:** A passive "Cold Brew Station" mechanic that brews high-value coffee over 2 hours of real-time, continuing even when the app is closed.

### 💾 Persistence
* **Autosave:** Progress, cash, inventory, and stats are saved automatically to `LocalStorage`.
* **History log:** View your last 10 orders to track wins, losses, and earnings.

## ⌨️ QWERTY control

The game is fully playable via the physical keyboard, optimized for speed and muscle memory:

### Navigation & Core
| Key | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| <kbd>SPACE</kbd> | Customer ↔ Kitchen ↔ Storage |
| <kbd>↵</kbd> | Serve drink |
| <kbd>⌫</kbd> | Clearcup |
| <kbd>ESC</kbd> | Close popups |

### Kitchen Ingredients
| Key | Ingredient | Key | Ingredient |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| <kbd>1</kbd> / <kbd>2</kbd> / <kbd>3</kbd> | Roasts (Light / Med / Dark) | <kbd>I</kbd> | **I**ce |
| <kbd>M</kbd> / <kbd>W</kbd> | **M**ilk / **W**ater | <kbd>F</kbd> | **F**oam |
| <kbd>C</kbd> / <kbd>A</kbd> | **C**hocolate / c**a**ramel | <kbd>T</kbd> | Matcha |
| <kbd>H</kbd> | C**h**ai | <kbd>N</kbd> | To**n**ic |
| <kbd>B</kbd> / <kbd>K</kbd> | **B**atch Brew / Cold Brew | | |

## 📥 Installation

1.  Download the APK file.
2.  Install on your **Minimal Phone**.
3.  Enjoy!

### ⚠️ About browser caching
The **Cold Brew Station** uses your device's system time. You can start a batch, close the app, and come back 2 hours later to collect your yield. Make sure not to clear your browser cache, or your brew progress will be lost!
